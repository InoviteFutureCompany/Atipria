Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Who we are

Inovitefuture ("we", "us", "our") operates our website and applications. We decide why and how your personal data is processed, which makes us the data controller under the EU and UK GDPR, a business under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the equivalent role under other privacy laws that apply to you.

  • Contact for privacy questions and requests: inovitefutureteam@gmail.com
  • Where we are based: Israel

If you are in the EEA or the UK and we do not have an establishment there, you may contact us at the address above and we will route your request to our representative.

3. Where the data comes from

  • Directly from you — when you create an account, fill in a form, make a purchase or contact support.
  • Automatically — through cookies, server logs and the SDKs listed in section 7 when you use the service.
  • From third parties — from the payment and infrastructure providers listed below.

5. AI and automated decision-making

We do not use your personal data to train AI models, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

8. International transfers

Some of the providers above process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or the provider's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and we assess the destination country's laws before we do so. For transfers out of mainland China we rely on the mechanisms permitted by the PIPL.

9. How we protect your data

  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it, on the principle of least privilege.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure providers.
  • A documented incident process: if a breach is likely to put you at risk we notify the competent authority within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay.

No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

12. Children

The service is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe someone below that age has given us data, write to inovitefutureteam@gmail.com and we will delete it. Where we know a user is under 16 we do not sell or share their data without opt-in consent, as California and EU member state law require.

13. Regional information

Other regions

14. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when what we do changes. The date at the top always reflects the current version, and previous versions are kept for audit. If a change is material we tell you by email or an in-app notice at least 30 days before it takes effect.

15. Real‑Time Monitoring and Data Processing

We monitor every action a user takes on the page and the content displayed in real‑time to enforce preferences. Activity data is processed and may be temporarily stored during the session in Firebase for authentication and analytics, and is not retained after the session ends.

16. Account Termination and Data Deletion

If an account is terminated for abuse or fraud, we immediately delete the associated personal data in accordance with our global retention baseline.

17. Legal Bases For EU And UK Users

For users in the EEA and the UK, every use of personal data rests on a legal basis under Article 6 of the GDPR: performance of our contract with you, compliance with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests (such as securing and improving the service), or your consent for anything optional. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time, and where we rely on legitimate interests you can object.

18. Exercising Your GDPR Rights

You can ask us for access to your personal data, rectification of anything inaccurate, erasure (the right to be forgotten), restriction of processing, and a portable machine-readable copy of the data you gave us. Send the request to the contact address in this policy or through our data request form; we verify it against your account, answer within one month, and never charge for a first request.

19. Data Protection Officer And EU Representative

Where Article 37 of the GDPR requires one, we designate a Data Protection Officer reachable through the contact address in this policy. If we are established outside the EU or the UK, users there may request the contact details of our Article 27 EU representative or UK representative at the same address, and may raise any matter with them directly.

20. Transfers Outside The EEA And UK

When personal data leaves the EEA or the UK, the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision, by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with the UK Addendum or IDTA, or by the provider's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can request a copy of the safeguard covering any transfer at the contact address in this policy.

21. Do Not Sell Or Share My Personal Information

California residents can opt out of any sale of personal information and of sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising through the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on our site or by contacting us. We honour the Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a valid opt-out, and we never treat you differently for exercising it.

22. Notice At Collection For California Residents

At or before the point of collection, California residents are entitled to know the categories of personal information we collect, the purposes we use each category for, whether it is sold or shared, and how long we keep it. Those disclosures are the data, purpose and retention sections of this policy, which together serve as our notice at collection under the CPRA.

23. Limit The Use Of Sensitive Personal Information

Where we collect sensitive personal information as the CPRA defines it, we use it only to provide the service you asked for and for the purposes section 7027(m) permits. California residents may direct us to limit its use or disclosure beyond those purposes at any time, using the contact address in this policy.

24. Additional US State Privacy Rights

Residents of Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA) and Utah (UCPA) can confirm whether we process their personal data, access and correct it, delete it, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. If we refuse a request you can appeal through the same contact route, and we will explain the outcome.

25. Global Retention Baseline

Wherever you live, we keep personal data only as long as the purpose it was collected for requires, plus any period a law obliges us to keep it, and then delete or anonymise it. This is the standard the LGPD in Brazil, PIPEDA in Canada, the Australian Privacy Act and the APPI in Japan each require, and we apply it to everyone.

26. Security Safeguards And Breach Notification

We protect personal data with technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity — encryption in transit, access controls, and vendor agreements. If a breach creates a real risk of harm we notify the competent authority and the affected users without undue delay, as the GDPR, the LGPD, PIPEDA, Australia's Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and the APPI each require.

27. Automated Decisions And Profiling Opt-Out

Where a decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you is made by automated means — including profiling — you have the right to opt out, to obtain human review, to express your point of view and to contest the outcome, under GDPR Article 22 and the CPRA's automated decision-making rules. Ask through the contact address in this policy and a person will review the decision.

28. AI Input Training Rules

Content you submit to AI features is sent to the model providers this policy names, under agreements that state whether it may be used to train their models and how long prompts and outputs are retained. Where training on your content occurs at all, this policy says so explicitly and an opt-out is available through the contact address; absent that statement, your inputs are used only to produce your output.
